How Ordinary People Waste Their Lives

普通人是如何一步步浪费一生的

•

Most people do not waste their lives because they never work hard. They waste them waiting, hesitating, and avoiding what matters. Stop waiting for life to improve on its own. Start doing what matters, one step at a time.

•

普通人浪费一生，往往不是因为不够努力，而是因为把时间花在等待、犹豫和逃避上。别再等生活自动变好，从今天开始，把真正重要的事情一件件做好。

Part 3 Discussion Prompt

"Why do so many educated people chronically postpone significant life decisions despite having access to more information than ever before?"

Cambridge 8.5+ Model Response

"Frankly speaking, modern delay is rarely a product of laziness; rather, it stems from the illusion of preparation. People often confuse being busy with moving forward, convincing themselves that gathering another credential or waiting for absolute certainty will mitigate risk. In reality, waiting for conditions to feel 'completely safe' is a cognitive trap. Growth inherently demands acting in the presence of discomfort, rather than postponing what matters until life is convenient."
